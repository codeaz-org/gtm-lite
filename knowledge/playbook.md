# Marketing playbook (project knowledge base)

A distillation of the ideas behind the Marketing-for-Engineers collection,
kept as agent-readable principles. Extend this per project — the pipeline
reads knowledge/ files, so what you add here shapes what the AI produces.

## Order of operations for a new product
1. **User research before channels.** Know who the user is, what they use
   today, and the words they use to describe the pain. Personas come from
   real conversations, not brainstorms.
2. **Do things that don't scale.** First 100 users come from manual,
   personal outreach — exactly what this repo automates the *prep* for.
3. **Pick one channel, go deep.** Content, communities, Product Hunt,
   or founder-led outbound. Master one before adding another.
4. **Optimize on quality of replies, not volume of sends.** One
   "send me more" beats ten opens. Feed winning messages back into
   knowledge/ so the next campaign is sharper.

## Channel notes
- **Content:** write for the reader's problem, not your feature list.
  Every post needs a distribution plan before it's written.
- **Communities (Reddit/HN/Discord):** contribute 10x more than you promote.
  Comment marketing works; drive-by links get you banned.
- **Product Hunt:** it's a 24-hour campaign with weeks of prep — decide if
  the audience overlap justifies the effort.
- **Outbound (this repo):** signal-based beats spray-and-pray. A lead who
  just launched, raised, hired, or complained publicly is worth 50 from a
  purchased list.

## Compounding loop
After each batch of sends, record in this folder:
- messages that got positive replies (verbatim, with why you think it worked)
- objections heard and the responses that re-engaged
- which briefs/segments produced the best fit_scores
The drafting model reads these files. This is the asset that compounds.
